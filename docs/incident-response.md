# Incident response

For credential exposure, unauthorized advisor access, RLS/cross-user failure,
malicious upload, exposed export, account takeover, corruption, storage failure,
or outage:

1. Detect and open a restricted incident record.
2. Contain affected traffic, grants, credentials, objects, or deployments.
3. Rotate exposed credentials and invalidate sessions or signed links.
4. Preserve privacy-safe evidence and establish affected scope.
5. Escalate to security, privacy, engineering, and communications owners.
6. Recover from a verified release or isolated restore.
7. Validate authorization, RLS, storage, and smoke tests before reopening.
8. Conduct a post-incident review with corrective actions.

Legal notification and user communication decisions require designated privacy
and legal owners; this runbook does not claim legal compliance.
